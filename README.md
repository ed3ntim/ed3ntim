## Selected work

[![cs.weapon](https://img.shields.io/badge/cs.weapon-18181b?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ed3ntim/cs.weapon) ![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/ed3ntim/cs.weapon?style=flat-square&label=Release) ![Status: release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-release-22c55e?style=flat-square)  
Blender add-on: spawn CS2 weapons, knives and gloves with any skin, pattern seed and float, rendered by the game's own paint shaders.

[![cs.sticker](https://img.shields.io/badge/cs.sticker-18181b?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ed3ntim/cs.sticker) ![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/ed3ntim/cs.sticker?style=flat-square&label=Release) ![Status: release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-release-22c55e?style=flat-square)  
Blender add-on: place CS2 stickers on meshes with finish-accurate materials (Paper, Glitter, Holo, Foil, Gold, Lenticular, Embroidered).

[![cs.charm](https://img.shields.io/badge/cs.charm-18181b?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ed3ntim/cs.charm) ![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/ed3ntim/cs.charm?style=flat-square&label=Release) ![Status: release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-release-22c55e?style=flat-square)  
Blender add-on: spawn CS2 charms (keychains) with their patterns.

[![forcebuy-release](https://img.shields.io/badge/forcebuy--release-18181b?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ed3ntim/forcebuy-release) ![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/ed3ntim/forcebuy-release?style=flat-square&label=Release) ![Status: beta](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-beta-f59e0b?style=flat-square)  
ForceBuy for Windows, macOS and Linux: a CS2 inventory and market ledger that matches every purchase and sale to its item. Read-only. Web beta at forcebuy.net.

[![forcebuy-connect](https://img.shields.io/badge/forcebuy--connect-18181b?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/ed3ntim/forcebuy-connect) ![Status: beta](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-beta-f59e0b?style=flat-square)  
ForceBuy Connect: browser extension that keeps your Steam access in ForceBuy fresh. Read-only mirror of the released source.
